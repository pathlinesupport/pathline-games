PATHLINE Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the PATHLINE mobile game ("the App"), published by the PATHLINE team ("we"), handles information. In short, your game data stays on your device. The App is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, stars, hints, daily-challenge history and settings are stored only on your device. We have no accounts or servers, and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the App deletes it. You can also delete it at any time in Settings → Reset game progress.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or microphone or camera data. The App does not request permission to access any of these.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows occasional full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, or the iOS IDFV and IDFA where permitted
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions, such as impressions and clicks, plus app and device information

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices and consent

Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and certain US states, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before showing personalised ads. If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to a family-friendly (PG) content rating.

Data security and retention

Game data never leaves your device, so we do not retain it. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
